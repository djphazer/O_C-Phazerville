Quad Viewer help

Connect the module to your computer — use the front-panel USB-C where fitted (XLOC2 or other Expanded ORN8 modules), otherwise the Teensy's micro-USB on the board — then click Connect and pick its serial port. The USB host jack is for MIDI controllers; the viewer can't connect through it. The grid shows four panels — use each panel's dropdown to choose what it shows (live screen, audio levels, scopes, MIDI monitor…) and ⛶ to fill the screen with one panel.

Classic switches to a single big mirror of the module screen. Control opens on-screen buttons and encoders so you can drive the module from here. Color cycles the phosphor tint, Save PNG captures the current view, and Fullscreen is handy for recording. Click MODE in the toolbar to switch the module between Quadrants and Audio Setup.

The preset dropdown chooses a slot; picking one changes nothing on the module. Load loads the chosen slot, and Save writes the current state into it.
